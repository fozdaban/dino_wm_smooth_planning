Dataset: DINO-WM "Wall" offline dataset (wall_single), released by the DINO-WM authors.
Not created by me and not included in this repository.

  Download: https://osf.io/bmw48/?view_only=a56a296ce3b24cceaf408383a175ce28  (datasets -> wall_single)
  Paper:    G. Zhou, H. Pan, Y. LeCun, L. Pinto, "DINO-WM: World Models on Pre-trained
            Visual Features enable Zero-shot Planning", ICML 2025.

Setup: unzip so that the folder $DATASET_DIR/wall_single exists, then
  export DATASET_DIR=/path/to/data

Only needed for `python main.py demo` (and plan.py): the planner reads the action/state
normalization statistics from it. `main.py test`, `toy` and `analyze` need no dataset;
`analyze` uses the per-episode results saved in results/ (included).
